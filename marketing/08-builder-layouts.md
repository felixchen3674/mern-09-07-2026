# Builder Layouts

The three layouts offered by the Skill Section Builder, shown left to right in this order: **most compact → balanced (default) → most detailed**. Each maps its categories onto the buckets of [07-technical-skills-bank.md](07-technical-skills-bank.md) (S01–S16).

Three is deliberate: enough that resumes don't all look alike, few enough that nobody freezes at the choice. (The wider set of 12 layouts in [06-skill-section-layouts.md](06-skill-section-layouts.md) maps to the full keyword bank and isn't used by the builder.)

The student can also choose the **category names** mode (Exact = copy the names as written; Creative = the AI picks its own wording), so two students on the same layout can still get different results. Line length is fixed in the prompt (roughly 10 to 15 skills per category).

The builder reads the `### N · Name` headings, the *italic* description, the category tables and the Quick picker at the bottom. Keep those shapes. Put `(default)` after the name of the layout that should be pre-selected.
