# Experience Bullets

How many bullets the **Experience Builder** asks the AI to write for one job, and which areas they cover. The company, title, dates and location are **not** generated: the student formats that line themselves.

Unlike the summary, this follows the student's actual work. The AI weights the areas by what the student described, and never invents work to fill an area. The total stays at 10 to 12 bullets.

The technologies come from [05-master-keyword-bank.md](05-master-keyword-bank.md) (the granular keywords: hooks, interceptors, indexing, and so on), with the conceptual-only (†) items left out. Whatever the student writes in their own description always counts too.

The builder reads the `### N · Label` headings and the `Bullets`, `Buckets` and `Covers` lines under each. Keep that shape.
